No private source, evidence, credential or absolute path is shown.

candidate validation, explicit Apply, verification and rollback. Screen 10 is a `TEST-ONLY PREVIEW`
of the native tray and is not native screenshot evidence. Screen 11 shows the in-app destination a

downloading, rejected and completed documentation; the actual transition proof is the updater JSON,
not four physical native captures. Screens 17–19 reference Diagnostics for package, signing and
performance evidence; exact values live in the reports. Screens 20–21 show Hebrew RTL Diagnostics
and the Hebrew product destination. A physical macOS notification or tray click is not depicted.
